For a long time, the SRE team relied entirely on rigid, hard-coded scripts. Their automation could only handle problems they could completely predict. If a strange new edge case appeared, the scripts broke, and the team had to fix them manually.The breakthrough came when they shifted to an agentic perspective. Instead of writing step-by-step code, the engineer learned how to build smart automation powered by AI reasoning. They turned standard operational tasks into tools and taught the system how to choose the right tool based on the problem it encountered.Seeing the AI successfully navigate unexpected errors in a test environment completely transformed the engineer’s confidence and autonomy. They realized they were no longer just maintaining old infrastructure, but creating smart systems that could think on their feet. As they put it: "Instead of just writing scripts to handle the problems I can predict, I now know how to build systems that can reason through the ones I can't."This successful transition changed their career direction. By mastering AI-driven workflows, they moved away from routine script maintenance and earned the opportunity to lead the team's next big AI project.
